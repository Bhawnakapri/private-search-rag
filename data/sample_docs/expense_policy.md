# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted later than 30 days will be rejected unless a director approves an exception.

A receipt is mandatory for any expense above 500 rupees. Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to 1200 rupees per day. Air travel must be booked in economy class, and train travel in AC 2-tier or lower, unless the trip exceeds 8 hours.

Your line manager approves the claim first, and then finance verifies the receipts. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll cycle. Personal expenses, fines, and alcohol are never reimbursed.
