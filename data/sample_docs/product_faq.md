# Product FAQ

How do I reset my password? Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page, enter your registered email, and follow the link we send you. The link expires after 30 minutes.

Can I export my data? Yes. Go to Settings, choose Data, and click Export to download all your records as a CSV file. Large exports are emailed to you when ready.

What plans are available? The Free plan includes 3 projects. The Pro plan costs 999 rupees per month and includes unlimited projects and priority support. Customer support is available from 9 AM to 6 PM IST, Monday to Friday.
