# Leave Policy

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month, so a new joiner earns leave from the first month of employment. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward into the next calendar year; anything above that lapses on 31 December.

Sick leave is 12 days per year. A doctor's note is required if the absence lasts more than 3 consecutive days.

To request annual leave, submit the request in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance and wait for your manager's approval. The list of public holidays is published by HR every January. Maternity leave is 26 weeks and paternity leave is 2 weeks, both fully paid.
