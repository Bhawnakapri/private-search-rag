# Incident Response Runbook

Incidents are classified by severity. SEV1 means a full outage or data loss affecting customers. SEV2 means a major feature is degraded. SEV3 means a minor issue with a workaround.

The on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 15 minutes. For a SEV1, open a dedicated incident channel immediately, assign an incident commander, and post a status update every 30 minutes until the issue is resolved.

After resolution, the incident commander writes a blameless postmortem within 5 business days. The postmortem must include a timeline, the root cause, and action items with owners and due dates.
