# VPN Setup Guide

Install the company VPN client from the IT self-service portal. Sign in with your single sign-on account and approve the multi-factor authentication prompt on your phone.

If the connection fails, first check that your laptop clock is correct, because certificate checks fail when the time is wrong. Then restart the client and try again. The VPN disconnects automatically after 8 hours of inactivity, and you will need to sign in again.

You can only reach internal tools such as the wiki and the build server while connected. For any problem that you cannot fix yourself, contact the IT helpdesk on extension 4100 or raise a ticket in the portal. Helpdesk hours are 8 AM to 8 PM on working days.
