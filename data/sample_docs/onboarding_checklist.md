# New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

On day one, collect your laptop from IT and set up your accounts for email, chat, and the code repository. Every new employee must complete the mandatory security awareness training within the first 5 working days.

You will be assigned an onboarding buddy who will answer day-to-day questions and introduce you to the team. During the first week, meet your manager to agree a 30-60-90 day plan with clear goals for each period.

By the end of the first month, finish the compliance modules in the learning portal and submit your tax declaration form to HR. A probation review is held after 90 days to confirm your role.
